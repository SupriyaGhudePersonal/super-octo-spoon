The train to Millbrook ran eleven minutes late every Tuesday, and nobody could explain why. The conductor blamed the signals, the signal operator blamed the weather, and the weather didn’t say anything. In the third carriage an old man peeled an orange in one long spiral while a little girl across the aisle watched like it was a magic trick. When the peel finally dropped into his lap, unbroken, she clapped. He bowed slightly, handed her a segment, and went back to his newspaper. Outside, the fields went from green to gold to green again, and the train picked up speed as though it suddenly remembered where it was going.
