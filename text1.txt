The lighthouse keeper’s cat had opinions about the weather. On foggy mornings it sat by the brass telescope and refused to move, as if it were the one in charge of the shipping lanes. Down in the harbor, fishermen argued over the price of mackerel while gulls circled overhead and waited for something to go wrong. By noon the fog had lifted, the cat had lost interest, and someone had left a half-finished crossword on the pier. Seven across was still blank: “a feeling of quiet contentment,” nine letters.
